EverReach — Personal CRM for Founders, Creators, and Networkers

A lightweight personal CRM that captures contacts from anywhere (screenshots, voice memos, calendar invites, LinkedIn DMs, emails) and uses AI to generate follow-up reminders with full context — what you talked about, when you last met, what to say next.

Target: solo operators who meet 20+ new people per month and lose track of who to follow up with. Founders raising rounds, creators with sponsor pipelines, salespeople managing informal contact networks.

Differentiator from existing personal CRMs (Dex, Clay, Folk, Monica): AI-drafted follow-ups grounded in your own saved notes, not generic templates. Treats screenshots and voice memos as first-class inputs, not afterthoughts.
